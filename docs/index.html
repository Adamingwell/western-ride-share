How it works

Request or offer a seat

Choose your town and travel days. For a trip to campus, enter when you need to arrive. For a trip home, enter when you can leave.

Find rides that fit

Filter by town and direction. Post the trip to campus and the trip home separately.

Coordinate privately

Use Messages to agree on a pickup spot and confirm the ride with the other person. A post alone does not reserve a seat.

Access and privacy

Who can access the ride board?

Register with a @westerntc.edu or @students.westerntc.edu email address, then confirm it to access the ride board.

What information is shared?

Signed-in members can see a post’s route, days, time, and whether it’s a request or offer. Ride listings do not show your name or contact details. Messages are private between the two participants.

How do I block someone or report a concern?

Use Block or Report from a ride post or conversation. Blocking stops new messages between the two accounts. Safety reports go to the site administrator.

Is this a Western or SMRT service?

Western Ride Share is an independent, student-led carpool board for Western Technical College’s La Crosse campus. The board covers 14 communities served by SMRT. Riders and drivers arrange their own trips.
